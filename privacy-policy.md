# Privacy Policy for E240

**Last Updated: January 13, 2025**

## Introduction

E240 is a VHS-style camera app. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to privacy and data.

## What We Collect

### No Personal Data Collection
- **Your Videos**: All videos and photos you create are stored only on your device. We never upload or access them.
- **No Account Required**: The app doesn't require registration or login.

### Analytics Only
We use minimal analytics to improve the app:
- Basic usage statistics (which features are popular)
- Crash reports to fix bugs
- Device type and iOS version for compatibility

These are collected anonymously through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

## Subscriptions

- Subscriptions are handled entirely through Apple's App Store
- We use Adapty to manage subscription status
- We don't store payment information

## Your Data, Your Control

- All your content stays on your device
- Delete the app to remove all app data
- Manage subscriptions in your iPhone Settings
- Control camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings

## Third-Party Services

The app uses:
- **Firebase**: Anonymous analytics and crash reporting
- **Adapty**: Subscription management
- **Apple**: App Store and payment processing

## Children's Privacy

E240 is rated 4+ on the App Store. We don't collect personal information from any users, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Check the "Last Updated" date for changes.
