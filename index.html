COUNTER 0:03 — QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

What is E240?

E240 (full App Store name: VHS Cam – E240) is a VHS camera app for iPhone. It records video that looks shot on a real 90s camcorder, VHS-ifies videos you already have, and plays memory tapes cut from your photo library on a VCR home screen.

How is E240 different from other VHS camera apps?

Most VHS apps composite a grainy overlay on top of clean footage. E240 processes the image the way a real VHS camera and tape did — the degradation comes from the signal itself, which is why the result reads as genuine camcorder footage instead of a filter.

Is E240 free?

Yes — free to download and use. An optional PRO subscription unlocks unlimited recording and the custom tape builder.

Can I put the VHS effect on videos I already filmed?

Yes. Import any clip into the editor, pick a tape, and export it back — with a burned-in timestamp set to any date you want.

Is E240 available for Android?

No — E240 is an iPhone app, available exclusively on the Apple App Store.
