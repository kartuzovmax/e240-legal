COUNTER 0:04 — QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

What is E240?

E240 (full App Store name: VHS Cam – E240) is a VHS camera app for iPhone. It records video that looks like it was shot on a real 90s camcorder — tape noise, color bleed, burned-in timestamp — and can apply the same treatment to videos already in your library.

How is E240 different from other VHS camera apps?

Most VHS apps composite a grainy overlay on top of clean footage. E240 processes the image the way a real VHS camera and tape did — the degradation comes from the signal itself, which is why the result reads as genuine camcorder footage instead of a filter.

Is E240 free?

Yes — E240 is free to download and use. An optional PRO subscription unlocks unlimited recording, all filters, custom timestamps, and removes watermarks.

Can I put the VHS effect on videos I already filmed?

Yes. Import any video from your photo library, run it through E240, and export it back with the full VHS treatment — timestamp included.

Can I change the date on the timestamp?

Yes. Set any custom date and time — the timestamp is burned into the footage exactly like a real camcorder OSD, so today's video can say JUN.09 1994.

Is E240 available for Android?

No — E240 is an iPhone app, available exclusively on the Apple App Store.
